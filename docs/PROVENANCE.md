No new sampler, heat-bath method or speed theorem is claimed. The related universal binary-matrix/bipartite row-pair gap is already in [Fu–Qin–Wang2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.22636v2). Prior [2018 bipartite comparison](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.APPROX-RANDOM.2018.36), [P-stable switch-chain classes](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejc.2021.103421), [SEA2026 empirical diagnostics](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.SEA.2026.2), and [NetworKit trade-count APIs](https://networkit.github.io/dev-docs/python_api/randomization.html) constrain broad novelty claims. GlobalCurveball's default20rounds are empirically motivated; this tool does not transfer the ordinary-chain bound to global rounds.

No equally general practical undirected epsilon wrapper surfaced in the bounded primary-source search. That finding is scoped and global priority remains unestablished.

This graph-resampling work is separate from the simplex-product dimension13 volume optimum. The geometric theorem is not a runtime, compression or graph-sampling guarantee.
