# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — research prototype

- Strict CSV and whitespace edge-list ingestion with exact label mapping and explicit isolated vertices.
- Conditional finite-TV planning for the existing ordinary undirected Curveball sampler.
- Caller-selected number of outputs, whole-batch error budget and conservative overall alpha target.
- One-thread bounded jobs, atomic streamed graph files, cancellation and full-output replay on resume.
- Input, degree, source, version, certificate and saved-file integrity checks.
- Plain-English local HTML/text reports and machine-readable metadata.
- Synthetic friendship example, exact small-state tests and operational tests.
- Apache-2.0 authored source; separate dependency licenses and AI assistance disclosed.

No new sampler, speed theorem, firstness, or unconditional end-to-end formal verification is claimed.
